Treatment built around how termites actually enter a building

Every structure is different — a new foundation, an occupied home, a warehouse — so the treatment method changes with it.

Pre-construction treatment

Chemical soil barrier applied at foundation stage, before slabs and columns are poured, so the structure is protected from the ground up.

Post-construction treatment

Drilling and chemical injection around walls, floors and entry points for buildings that are already occupied or completed.

Wood borer treatment

Targeted treatment for furniture, doors, roof timber and other wood-boring insect damage beyond termites alone.

Termite baiting systems

In-ground bait stations for sites where drilling isn't practical, monitored on a set schedule until activity stops.

Annual maintenance contracts

Scheduled re-inspection and touch-up treatment so early activity is caught before it becomes structural damage.

Commercial & industrial sites

Larger-scale treatment for warehouses, factories and offices, scheduled around working hours and operations.

What happens after you call

The same four stages, whether it's one room or a full construction site.

Site inspection

A technician checks for live activity, mud tubes, moisture points and likely entry paths around the structure.

Treatment plan

Method and chemical are matched to the building type — soil barrier, drilling, or bait stations.

Application

Treatment is carried out with attention to occupied spaces, pets and ventilation.

Monitoring & warranty

Follow-up visits confirm the treatment held, with warranty coverage on the work.
